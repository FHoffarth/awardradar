# AwardRadar

**Find miles. Fly better.**

AwardRadar is an intelligent travel search platform for deal hunters, frequent flyers, and award travelers.

Instead of only showing available flights, AwardRadar helps users identify the **best overall value**, combining:

* 💸 Cheap Flights
* 🕳️ Hidden-City (Skiplag) Opportunities
* 🎯 Award Travel
* 🔥 Sweet Spot Analysis

---

## Vision

Most flight search engines answer:

> Which flights are available?

AwardRadar aims to answer:

> Which flight is the smartest deal?

By combining cash fares, hidden-city opportunities, and award travel options into a single interface, AwardRadar helps travelers make better booking decisions.

---

## Current Features

### 💸 Cheap Flights

Search for affordable flights worldwide.

Current and planned data sources:

* Travelpayouts
* Amadeus
* Google Flights
* Skyscanner
* Kiwi
* Additional flight search providers

---

### 🕳️ Hidden-City Radar

Identify potential hidden-city ticket opportunities.

Example:

Direct itinerary:

FRA → HKG
€799

Hidden-city itinerary:

FRA → HKG → MNL
€462

Potential savings:

€337

---

### 🎯 Award Search

Quick access to major loyalty programs and award booking sources.

Planned integrations:

* Miles & More
* United MileagePlus
* Aeroplan
* Singapore KrisFlyer
* British Airways Executive Club (Avios)

---

### 🔥 Sweet Spot Engine

Evaluate whether cash or miles provide the better value.

Example:

Cash Fare:

€920

Award Fare:

35,000 miles + €120

Value:

2.29 cents per mile

Result:

🔥 SWEET SPOT
