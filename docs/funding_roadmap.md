# AwardRadar Funding Roadmap

## Executive Summary

AwardRadar is building an operating system for frequent flyers.

The platform combines Booking Intelligence, Status Intelligence, and Traveler Intelligence to help travelers make better booking and loyalty decisions.

Funding should be treated as a stack, not as a single grant application:

1. Low-friction cloud and AI credits
2. Brand and IP foundation
3. Research tax credit eligibility
4. BAFA consulting support
5. KfW, WIBank, and Hessen financing
6. Ecosystem access and later-stage programs

This roadmap is a planning document. Program details, amounts, deadlines, eligibility, and application rules must be verified against the official sources before applying.

## Commercial Story

### Problem

Frequent flyers face fragmented systems:

- award availability
- loyalty programs
- status rules
- transfer partners
- cash fares
- journey complexity
- changing program rules

These systems are hard to compare and often force users into manual spreadsheet work, forum research, and multi-tab searches.

### Solution

AwardRadar provides a unified decision layer that transforms fragmented travel and loyalty data into actionable recommendations.

The core product question is:

> Should I book this?

The long-term product expands that question into:

> What should I fly next?

### Market

AwardRadar addresses global premium travel, loyalty programs, and frequent flyer ecosystems, with an initial DACH and Miles & More / Star Alliance wedge.

The first market advantage is local expertise:

- Lufthansa Group relevance
- Miles & More-first logic
- DACH airport and rail/feeder context
- German and European user needs

## Technology Story

Core technology areas:

- award search aggregation
- cash vs. miles valuation
- ranking and recommendation engine
- personalization
- traveler intelligence
- AI orchestration
- booking decision engine
- journey intelligence
- status intelligence

Potential research and development narrative:

AwardRadar is not merely displaying travel data. It is combining volatile availability, market prices, loyalty rules, traveler preferences, and confidence signals into explainable booking and status recommendations.

## Ecosystem Story

AwardRadar has a strong fit with the Hessen and Frankfurt ecosystem:

- Frankfurt is a major aviation, mobility, and finance hub.
- Lufthansa Group and Star Alliance relevance create a natural DACH wedge.
- HOLM Frankfurt is relevant for aviation, mobility, and logistics connections.
- StartHub Hessen can support funding navigation and ecosystem onboarding.
- Later-stage programs can support internationalization.

Potential ecosystem partners:

- airlines
- loyalty programs
- travel platforms
- startup ecosystems
- cloud providers
- accelerator programs
- mobility and aviation clusters

## Research Tax Credit Matrix

Potential R&D domains:

| Domain | Possible R&D Question | Evidence To Collect |
|---|---|---|
| Decision Engine | How can fragmented award, cash, and confidence signals be transformed into reliable booking recommendations? | Architecture notes, experiments, ranking changes, validation cases |
| Ranking Algorithms | How should results be ordered by real decision value rather than raw price or availability? | Scoring versions, test cases, before/after examples |
| Personalization | How can traveler-specific constraints change the optimal recommendation? | Traveler profile schemas, simulation cases, evaluation notes |
| AI Orchestration | How can LLMs explain and structure decisions without inventing facts? | Prompt/schema versions, tool-call traces, guardrail tests |
| Journey Intelligence | How can real-world origins, airports, rail, and feeder systems be mapped into searchable travel intent? | Dataset design, validation notes, model assumptions |
| Status Intelligence | How can status rules and planned travel be converted into optimal status strategy? | Rule models, calculation tests, scenario outputs |
| Recommendation Systems | How can AwardRadar measure whether a recommendation led to a better booking or status decision? | Metrics definitions, feedback loops, product analytics |

## Key Risks

- Wrong program fit can waste months.
- Do not rely on EIC, Eurostars, or EXIST for short-term runway.
- Avoid double-funding the same costs.
- Confirm whether a program forbids starting work before approval.
- Verify all program terms against official sources before applying.
- Do not submit IP or trademark applications without clearance.
